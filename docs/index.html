Core Capabilities

Designed for Modern Group Workflows

From weekend sports leagues to team decision making, PulsePoll brings clarity and speed to group events.

🔑

6-Character Join Codes

No messy account invites or lost links. Share a clean 6-character short code (e.g. SOC5V5) to instantly discover and vote on any device.

👥

Multi-User Social SSO

Switch identities seamlessly between Google, Facebook, and Apple test profiles to simulate real consensus, multi-device quorum tests, and instant rank updates.

🎟️

RSVP Headcount & +1s

Track exact attendee numbers with built-in guest calculations for venue reservations, team matches, and catering headcounts.

📊

Live Leaderboards & Quorum

Real-time visual percentage indicators, winner gold badges, and quorum threshold tracking to confirm when enough votes have been registered.

🚀

1-Tap Ready Templates

Pre-populated templates for sports pitches, Friday happy hours, dinner choices, camping trips, and sprint retros ready in a single tap.

📤

Multi-Format Export

Generate one-tap formatted summaries for WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram, and Discord, or download comprehensive CSV reports for spreadsheet tracking.
