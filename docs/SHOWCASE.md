# 🌟 PulsePoll Feature & User Experience Showcase

A comprehensive visual walkthrough and feature breakdown of **PulsePoll** — built for social groups, sports leagues, and team events.

---

## 📸 Screen-by-Screen Showcase

### 1. 🏠 Homepage & Clean Action Hub
* **Collapsible Insights Pill**: Tap `[⚡ 3 Active • 4 Polls ▼]` to reveal or hide the live consensus overview and deadlines countdown.
* **Instant Filter Chips**: Seamlessly filter between `All`, `Active`, `⚽ Soccer`, `🍻 Drinks`, `🍕 Food`, `🎉 Events`, and `🔒 Closed`.
* **Expandable Search**: Tap the search icon to instantly query polls by title, location, organizer name, or 6-character join code.
* **Social SSO Avatar Pill**: Shows current logged-in identity with Google/Facebook/Apple provider badges.

---

### 2. 🗳️ Voting & RSVP Screen
* **Multi-Vote & Single-Choice Modes**: Supports flexible election modes with configurable vote limits.
* **Real-Time Option Bars**: Visual feedback showing current vote counts and leading percentages before and after voting.
* **Integrated RSVP Attendance Selector**:
  * ✅ **Going (+ guests)**: Choose headcount (1 to 5+ attendees).
  * ❓ **Maybe**: Register tentative availability.
  * ❌ **Can't Make It**: Decline with real-time updates to group headcounts.
* **Quorum Progress Indicator**: Visual meter showing progress toward the required number of voters.

---

### 3. 📊 Real-Time Analytics & Quorum Dashboard
* **Dynamic Leaderboard**: Gold badge for the leading option with live vote totals and voter avatars.
* **Voter Attendance Roster**: Complete list of attendees with RSVP status, +1 headcounts, and timestamps.
* **Quorum Status Banner**: Real-time indication of whether quorum criteria have been met.
* **CSV & Formatted Text Export**: One-tap export of comprehensive poll results for event coordination.
