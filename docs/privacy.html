Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

LivePulse is a live polling and group voting app built by Plutusoft. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is stored, and how you can have it deleted. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run polls, we never sell anything, and there are no ads and no analytics.

What we collect

  • Account data — if you sign in with Google: your display name, email address, and a unique account ID. This comes from Google Sign-In and is stored by Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create — polls, options, votes, RSVPs, headcounts, ratings, and notes you submit, together with the display name and account ID (or guest ID) attached to them.

Guest mode: if you join with a display name instead of signing in, only that display name and a locally generated guest ID are used. No email is collected.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or services, and no sale or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, files, or microphone. The app requests no sensitive permissions.

Where data lives

Account data and poll content are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), transmitted over encrypted connections. Some data (your sign-in session, preferences, and drafts) is also kept locally on your device; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Deleting your data

Email khoa.trantan2000@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account and your polls, votes, and notes. Deleting a poll you own removes it and its votes for everyone.

Children

LivePulse is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact
